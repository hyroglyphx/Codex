## Convention clarification (audit request 3)

ρ_k here is the **affine** theory — equivalently the unreduced Burau representation at t = −1 acting on the full affine H_1. The reduced Burau at t = −1 acts on the compactified curve's H_1; for odd n the two ranks coincide (n−1 = 2g), while for even n the compact theory is exactly the radical quotient of the affine one. Nothing in SGT-007 mixes the two: its claims are stated and proved for the affine representation throughout, and the even-n quotient is correctly deferred.
