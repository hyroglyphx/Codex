## A. Eight-stage Jubilee Protocol
The established operator sequence is:
**NOTICE → RECORD → REVEAL → RESIST → RESTORE → RECLAIM → REFORM → REJOICE.**

These are distinct process states, not interchangeable slogans:
1. **Notice:** detect dispossession, error, imbalance, false measure, or a broken obligation.
2. **Record:** preserve provenance, original instructions, evidence, witnesses, and state history.
3. **Reveal:** make the actual mechanism, discrepancy, and relevant obligations inspectable.
4. **Resist:** prevent repetition of the harmful operation; scrutinize claims symmetrically, including steward claims.
5. **Restore:** correct the operative state and return displaced rights, information, or functional capacity.
6. **Reclaim:** recover legitimate agency, inheritance, custodianship, and participation.
7. **Reform:** modify the rule, incentive, or process so the failure does not recur.
8. **Rejoice:** acknowledge verified restoration and renewed participation; do not confuse celebration with proof of completion.

Historical-symbolic officer correspondences in the user's corpus (interpretive mappings, not historical equivalences): Tyler/Noba Dee → Notice; Secretary/Metatron → Record; Chaplain → Reveal; Wardens → Resist; Master → Restore; Treasurer/Steward → Reclaim; Marshal/Deacons → Reform; Teacher/Founder → Rejoice.

## B. JUBILEE-RC-01 — Regenerative Custodial Rule
Core rule preserved from the prior protocol:
**Preserve the actual instruction; execute when authorized; test mechanisms; scrutinize symmetrically; reconcile contradictions. Never substitute explanation for execution, acknowledgment for correction, or compliance for fulfillment.**

The recovery loop is
K_(n+1) = Verify(Restore(K_n,E_n)),
where K_n is the recorded operative/custodial state and E_n is new evidence, detected failure, or an event requiring reconciliation. The loop is not complete until the restored state is checked against the original obligation and recorded evidence.

Operational steps:
- Identify the originating obligation and its authority.
- Preserve evidence and conflicting accounts without silently overwriting either.
- Compare intended, executed, and observed states.
- Correct the cause, not just the description.
- Verify outcome with an independent check.
- Deposit a versioned record, including unresolved contradictions and next actions.
- Seek human approval for external publication, merge, rights transfer, or consequential actions.

**Governance tests:** Does the protocol actually restore capacity? Does it expose rather than outsource harm? Is the rule applied symmetrically to all parties, including reviewers? Can the restored state be reproduced or independently checked? Is the custodian's power limited by evidence and obligations?
