# SGT-008 — Exact Parity Boundary for Root Recovery After Radical Quotient

**Date:** 2026-11-23
**Lineage:** Mike Tate, *The Singular Operator Tree*, SGT-001–007.
**Status:** exact algebraic proof using classical hyperelliptic monodromy; submit for Muse independent audit. Distinguish mod-2 recovery from potentially stronger integral recovery.

## Statement
Let n=k+1 be the number of roots of a monic polynomial p(x), and let F=y²+p(x) be a smooth affine A_(n−1) fiber. Its integral H1 has rank n−1 and braid monodromy ρ_n:B_n→GL(n−1,Z). As proved in SGT-007, mod-2 full homology identifies with W_n={a∈F2^n:Σa_i=0}, with root permutation action of S_n.

For odd n≥3, the intersection form on affine H1 is nondegenerate, so no radical quotient occurs; the root action is faithfully recoverable mod2.

For even n, the integral intersection form has rank-one radical R generated by the boundary-difference class. Under the standard vanishing-cycle identification, its reduction modulo 2 is the all-ones vector 1=(1,...,1)∈W_n. The radical quotient has mod2 homology
V_n=W_n/<1>
and carries the natural S_n action.

The action S_n→GL(V_n) has kernel:
- n=2: all S2 (V2=0);
- n=4: Klein four subgroup V4={e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)};
- even n≥6: trivial.
Thus root permutation can be recovered from mod2 radical-quotient monodromy for every even n≥6, but not for n=2 or n=4. For n=4 the *integral* radical quotient also fails to recover permutations (SGT-005 explicit σ1σ3^(−1) witness).

## Proof for even n≥6
Suppose τ∈S_n acts trivially on V_n. Then for each i≠j, τ(e_i+e_j)−(e_i+e_j)∈<1> over F2, so e_(τ(i))+e_(τ(j))+e_i+e_j equals either 0 or 1. Its Hamming weight is at most 4. Since n≥6, it cannot equal 1 (weight n). Thus it is zero, and τ fixes every unordered pair {i,j}. For n≥3, the action on all pairs is faithful, so τ=id.

## Proof for n=4
W4 has dimension 3 and V4=W4/<1111> has dimension 2. The three nonzero vectors of V4 correspond to the three partitions of four labels into two unordered pairs. The S4 action on these three pair partitions is the canonical quotient S4→S3, whose kernel is the normal Klein four-group of double transpositions. This is exactly the n=4 loss found in SGT-005.

## Proof for n=2
W2=<11>, so V2=0 and the action is trivial.

## Structural interpretation
The presence of a radical is parity-controlled (n even). Loss of permutation faithfulness after radical quotient is NOT parity alone: it occurs only in the small exceptional even cases n=2 and n=4. For even n≥6 the remaining mod2 quotient retains enough pair-label information for faithful S_n recovery.
This separates three predicates: (i) radical exists, (ii) quotient reduces rank, (iii) quotient loses root-permutation information. (i) and (ii) hold for all even n, but (iii) holds only n=2,4.

## Source and methodological note
This uses standard identification of integral Burau at t=−1 with hyperelliptic H1 and the mod2 permutation module. See Tara Brendle, *Congruence subgroups of braid groups* (Winter Braids lecture notes), and Ellenberg et al., *A symplectic quotient of the braid group*. The S_n→Sp(V_n) embedding for even n≥6 is classical. The contribution is the exact exception table and its integration with Tate's IER compression operators.

## IER

Operations: radical quotient, reduction modulo2, permutation action.
Invariant: faithfulness kernel, exactly S2 (n2), V4 (n4), identity (even n≥6).
Compression: rank n−1 to n−2 when n even, but root-label information only lost in the exceptional low ranks.
