# SGT-010 — Exceptional ADE Local-Algebra Fingerprints

**Date:** 2026-11-23
**Lineage:** Mike Tate, *The Singular Operator Tree*, SGT-001–009.
**Status:** exact calculations from classical ADE normal forms; proposed atlas synthesis, not a claim of new ADE classification.
**Audit destination:** Muse.

## Statement
For isolated plane function singularities, let Q_f=C{x,y}/(f_x,f_y), m its maximal ideal, μ=dim_C Q_f, and H_f(j)=dim_C m^j/m^(j+1). The Hilbert vector H_f is an invariant of the isomorphism class of Q_f. The following exact fingerprints distinguish A_μ, D_μ, E_μ for μ=6,7,8.

| Type | f | μ | Hilbert vector |
| A6 | x^7+y² | 6 | (1,1,1,1,1,1) |
| D6 | x²y+y^5 | 6 | (1,2,1,1,1) |
| E6 | x³+y⁴ | 6 | (1,2,2,1) |
| A7 | x^8+y² | 7 | (1,1,1,1,1,1,1) |
| D7 | x²y+y^6 | 7 | (1,2,1,1,1,1) |
| E7 | x³+xy³ | 7 | (1,2,2,1,1) |
| A8 | x^9+y² | 8 | (1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1) |
| D8 | x²y+y^7 | 8 | (1,2,1,1,1,1,1) |
| E8 | x³+y⁵ | 8 | (1,2,2,2,1) |

## E6 computation
J=(3x²,4y³)=(x²,y³). Q=C[x,y]/(x²,y³). Monomial basis {1,x,y,xy,y²,xy²}. Degrees 0:1, 1:x,y, 2:xy,y², 3:xy². Thus H=(1,2,2,1), μ=6, embedding dimension 2, socle generated by xy².

## E8 computation
J=(3x²,5y⁴)=(x²,y⁴). Basis {1,x,y,xy,y²,xy²,y³,xy³}; degree slices 1; x,y; xy,y²; xy²,y³; xy³. Thus H=(1,2,2,2,1), μ=8, socle xy³.

## E7 computation
J=(3x²+y³,3xy²)=(x²+y³/3,xy²). Then x²=−y³/3, xy²=0. Multiplying first relation by y² gives y⁵=0; multiplying by x gives x³=0 (since xy³=0). A basis is {1,x,y,xy,y²,y³,y⁴}, μ=7. The m-adic filtration is:
m/m²: [x],[y] (dimension 2).
m²/m³: [xy],[y²] (dimension 2).
m³/m⁴: [y³] (dimension 1), noting y³=−3x² and is also in m³.
m⁴/m⁵: [y⁴] (dimension 1).
m⁵=0. Hence H=(1,2,2,1,1). The socle is generated by y⁴. Note that E7 is not homogeneous in the ordinary grading, so the m-adic associated graded must be used; naïve counting by polynomial degree would be misleading.

## D_k comparison (k≥5)
J=(2xy,x²+(k−1)y^(k−2)). Relations xy=0, x²=−(k−1)y^(k−2), y^(k−1)=0. Basis {1,x,y,...,y^(k−2)}; μ=k. m/m² has dimension 2; for j=2,...,k−2, m^j/m^(j+1) has dimension 1 (represented by y^j). Hence H=(1,2,1,...,1), with k−2 trailing ones after the first 2 entries (total dimension k). This gives the D6/D7/D8 entries in the table.

## A_k comparison
Q_A=C[x]/(x^k) and H=(1 repeated k times).

## Exact classification consequence
Within the list {A_μ,D_μ,E_μ} at μ=6,7,8, the pair (μ,H_f) distinguishes each of the three ADE labels. In fact the Hilbert vectors shown are already pairwise distinct. This does not claim the Hilbert function classifies arbitrary isolated singularities or all local Artin algebras; nonisomorphic algebras can share Hilbert functions.

## IER
Object: simple isolated plane-curve function singularities.
Partition: equal Milnor number μ.
Representation: Jacobian local Artin algebra.
Operations: maximal-ideal powers and associated graded.
Invariants: μ, embedding dimension, Hilbert vector, socle.
Compression: scalar μ discards multiplication structure; H recovers ADE distinction at μ=6,7,8.
Regeneration: exact presentations and monomial bases above.
