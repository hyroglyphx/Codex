## What was verified

1. **Radical generator → all-ones (request 1).** Under δ_i ↔ w_i, the alternating radical vector δ_1 + δ_3 + ⋯ maps to the telescoping sum of odd-indexed w_i, computed equal to (1,…,1) for n = 4, 6, 8. So V_n = W_n/⟨1⟩ is the correct mod-2 form of the integral radical quotient.

2. **n=4 kernel is exactly V_4 (request 2).** All 24 permutations computed: the quotient action S_4 → GL(2,2) has exactly 6 distinct matrices (image order 6 = |S_3| = |GL(2,2)|) and kernel exactly {e, (12)(34), (13)(24), (14)(23)}. This is the canonical S_4 → S_3 action on the three pair partitions, and it is precisely the loss SGT-005 found by hand.

3. **Even n≥6 faithful, no hidden n=6 exception (request 3).** Exhaustive kernels: n=6 (720 perms) trivial; n=8 (40,320 perms) trivial. The weight step of the proof was also exhibited at its failure point: at n=4, the pair {1,3} under (12)(34) has defect vector exactly 1 (weight 4 = n) — the single configuration the weight bound cannot exclude, and the reason n=4 is exceptional. For n≥6 every pair defect has weight ≤ 4 < n and can never equal 1. Additionally, for n = 4, 6, 8 the mod-2 form on W_n has radical exactly ⟨1⟩, the induced form on V_n is nondegenerate, and the quotient generators preserve it — the action genuinely lands in Sp(V_n), so "S_6 → Sp(4,2) faithful" is verified in form as well as in kernel.

4. **Odd n.** The all-ones vector is not in W_n for odd n (coordinate sum is odd — computed for n = 3, 5, 7), so the quotient does not exist there; consistent with the SGT-007 audit's radical dimension 0 for odd n. No quotient, no loss.

5. **B_n kernels (request 5).** By composition with SGT-007's verified factorization (the V_n action of B_n factors as B_n → S_n → GL(V_n)): the exact kernel in B_n is π_n⁻¹(V_4) at n=4, all of B_2 at n=2, and π_n⁻¹(e) = P_n for every even n≥6.
