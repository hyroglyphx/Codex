## Findings from actual passages
1. Maier, Emblem I, PDF p.8: one carrier/contained process is explicitly presented through chemical, embryological, arithmetic, musical, geometric and astronomical registers. The author asks the reader to compare the different subjects. Cross-domain comparison is native to this source, not solely our retrospective method.

2. Maier, Emblem XXI / Discourse XXI, PDF pp.67–69: circle→quadrangle→triangle→circle is explicitly explained through simple body, four elements/colours, body-spirit-soul, and stable redness/unification. The discourse discusses manual solid geometry and sphere/cube capacity. This supplies an ordered transformation model; it does not supply a modern area-preserving/conformal map. It also disputes a strong version of Platonic innate knowledge and privileges cultivation, reason and experience in the sampled discussion.

3. Vitruvius III.1, PDF pp.102–104 (printed 72–74): chosen-standard → part/whole proportion → coordinated construction. Circle/square body outlines and the rival perfections of ten and six are discussed; sixteen combines their metrological roles. His symmetria is commensuration/proportion, not automatically our letter-grid reflection group.

4. Dee Monas, Theorems 1–3, PDF p.12: point/line/circle are a constructive dependency chain, followed by their cosmological interpretation. Printer address p.11: preservation of letters, points, lines, figures, numbers and diagrams is explicitly requested. Formalizing construction dependencies is well motivated; equating Dee's point with an Abramelin fixed center remains a separate candidate bridge.

5. Pantheus, Voarchadumia, 1550 edition title and Intentio, PDF pp.15–17: distinguishes colouring metals while their underlying substance remains, sought transmutation, and the purification class introduced as Voarchadumia. p.16 lists textual/diagrammatic means of reading predecessors and invokes an Aristotle/Categories circle-quadrature example. This offers an independent appearance/substance discriminator for Maier's colour sequence. It is not evidence that every alchemical colour change meant the same operation, nor an established direct Pantheus→Maier transmission.

6. Vaughan, Magia Adamica within the bound scan, PDF pp.64–66 (printed 10–12): distinguishes the art/principles from their communicated/revealed history. He presents composition/resolution and a break in superior/inferior channels. Compare stable principles versus disrupted access/transmission. These are Vaughan's theological/historical assertions, not independently demonstrated ancient genealogy. The scan begins with The Man-Mouse; filename alone would misidentify its opening tract.

7. Soyga, Kupin edition, section 27, PDF pp.541–544 (printed 529–532): theoretical obscurity is said to become practical in tables; letters are assigned counts and functions; NISRAM binds four sides; successive named operations and undoing operations are paired under AGLA. This is a new primary-text source for ADDRESS→ACTION→COUNTERACTION structure. The source's letter-associated counts (e.g. A=3, B=18) must be kept separate from Reeds' modular lookup f and ordinal letter values. No equivalence between these number layers has been established.

8. Soyga PDF p.544, manuscript locator [102v–103r], presents TABULA prima ARIES and six visible rows. It provides a portable target-block candidate beyond Jethro's two short prefixes. Extracted text contains glyph ambiguities and discrepancies relative to the generator: retain them as a witness/OCR audit frontier until page-image inspection. ## New synthesis
The sampled texts support a comparison grammar:
reference/principle → differentiation/arrangement → permitted transformation → recombination/closure → judgment of preserved relation.
They also supply native tests of failure: false metallic appearance (Pantheus), damaged typography (Dee), missing modalities (Atalanta edition), mistaken access/history of principles (Vaughan), and unknown letter/number layer (Soyga).
This grammar is a modern synthesis. It is richer than a repeated numeral and narrower than a claim of one historical code.
