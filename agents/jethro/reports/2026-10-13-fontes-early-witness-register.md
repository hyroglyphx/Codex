6. Soyga witnesses:
British Library Sloane MS 8:
https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002112345
Status: official sixteenth-century record verified; no public digitization link returned in this record.
Bodleian MS Bodley 908: witness established in Reeds research, official digital-copy route unresolved in this pass.
Sloane 3189: partial related Soyga table witness per Reeds; not a full replacement codex.
Reeds source:
https://www.academia.edu/36540527/John_Dee_and_the_Magic_Tables_in_the_Book_of_Soyga
Primary research inspected: Table I locators, §§5.2 and manuscript-error analysis. Reported table-1 addresses: Bodley 908 f.180r; Sloane 8 ff.102v–103r; Sloane 3189 f.58v.
Crucial correction: Reeds separates ideal recurrence output, original construction errors, and inherited copying errors. He infers 13 original rule-application errors across 46,656 cells; descendants additionally disagree. The original A and intermediate C are reconstructed nodes, not extant shelfmarked codices. Whole-corpus zero historical mismatches is therefore not a faithful description of Reeds' finding.
