Group G = {identity, transpose, half-turn, anti-diagonal reflection}, acting
on (row, col) of an n×n grid. Enumerated by the script; horizontal/vertical
reflection is NOT in G (it is not generated by transpose + half-turn).
Transpose-only subgroup {identity, transpose} is carried for comparison.

Orbit sizes: corners form 4-cell orbits; edge non-corner cells form 4-cell
orbits; diagonal cells pair (i,i) ↔ (n−1−i,n−1−i) (2-cell orbits); the center
cell (n odd) is a 1-cell orbit. No orbit exceeds 4 cells in these squares.
