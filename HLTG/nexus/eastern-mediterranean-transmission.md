# Eastern Mediterranean Typed Transmission Network v0.1

Status: research graph proposal, not a claim of a single direct Ugarit–Aphek–Cyprus transmission chain.

## Nodes
- UGARIT: Late Bronze Age polyglot archive and scribal setting; RS 25.421, RS 94.2939, RS 20.123.
- APHEK: Late Bronze Age local lexical/prism and administrative context; Aphek 1 and Aphek 3.
- CYPRUS_LBA: Bronze Age Cypro-Minoan material including Enkomi; language unclassified.
- CYPRUS_IA: later Cypriot syllabary, Greek and Eteocypriot evidence; distinct chronology.
- HATTUSA: possible source/training context for RS 25.421.
- MESOPOTAMIAN_LEXICAL_TRADITION: textual/curricular tradition, **not a population or language ancestor**.
- AMARNA_CORRESPONDENCE: peripheral Akkadian scribal context with local West Semitic interference.

## Benchmark tasks
1. **COLUMN-ALIGN**: address exact Sumerian/Akkadian/Hurrian/Ugaritic/Hittite/Canaanite columns; distinguish translation, phonetic Sumerian, and broken/lost columns.
2. **MATERIAL-vs-TEXT**: local Aphek clay does not imply local invention of the lexical tradition; imported text does not prove imported object.
3. **ORTHOGRAPHY-vs-DIALECT**: learned Akkadian spelling variation at Ugarit must not be promoted to spoken language divergence without controls.
4. **CONTACT-vs-GENEALOGY**: translation across Hurrian/Semitic/Indo-European does not imply common ancestry.
5. **ROUTE-DIRECTION**: compare import, trained scribe, shared curriculum and independent adaptation; require discriminating provenance.
6. **CHRONOLOGY-GATE**: do not equate Bronze Age Cypro-Minoan and later Eteocypriot or reopen CYP-NEXUS family comparisons.
7. **NEGATIVE-CONTROL**: shared geographic corridor alone must fail the direct-borrowing test.
