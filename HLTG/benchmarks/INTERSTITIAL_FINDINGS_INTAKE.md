## Additional invariants
1. A physically aligned multilingual column set demonstrates deliberate equivalence-making, not language ancestry.
2. Material provenance and language provenance are separate.
3. A phonetic Sumerian column is a reading-layer witness, not another genetically independent language.
4. Internal family replication does not determine migration direction.
5. Unsupervised sign classification is not decipherment.
6. A claimed substrate is not automatically identifiable by exclusion.
7. Contradictions, absent signs, broken entries, unverified primary editions, and negative controls remain addressable.
