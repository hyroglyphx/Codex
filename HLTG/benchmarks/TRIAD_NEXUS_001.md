# TRIAD-NEXUS-001 — Lexicon, enacted mediation, narrated analogy

Status: **SOURCE-CHECKED COMPARATIVE DESIGN**, not a demonstrated historical chain or an executed statistical test.
Date: 2026-10-09.
Research question: what is preserved when knowledge changes carrier, audience and purpose?

## Three independently addressable witnesses
1. **APHEK3** — Late Bronze Age Aphek 3 trilingual liquids list, Sumerian / Akkadian / West Semitic (Canaanite). Example water `mu-mi`, wine `ye-nu`. Evidence type: artifact with aligned lexical columns; broken readings retained. Dossier: *Interstitial Findings.html*; contextual analysis https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7549s617 . Do not infer spoken fluency, translator identity, or successful real-world action.
2. **XEN.ANAB.4.5.33-36** — Xenophon reports gesture-mediated service (§33), Persian-speaking interpreter for Armenian village chief (§34), questions about territory, royal horse tribute, Chalybian neighbors and route, and the chief's instruction on bags for horses' feet in snow (§36). Text https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Aabo%3Atlg%2C0032%2C006%3A4%3A5 . Text reports the instruction, **not an independently observed successful later field test**; avoid overstating demonstrated efficacy. Date 4th c. BCE narrative of 401 BCE.
3. **LUC.DDS.60** — *On the Syrian Goddess* §60 compares dedication of hair at Hierapolis with Troezen/Hippolytus, specifies differing participants and details, and claims personal participation and an inscribed named vessel. Text https://www.philipharland.com/Blog/2022/07/syrian-perspectives-lucian-of-samosata-on-the-syrian-goddess-in-full-mid-second-century-ce/ . This is an **authorial report and analogy**, not an independently inspected surviving vessel; authorship and rhetorical positioning require source criticism. 2nd c. CE.

## Typed transforms and invariants
| Case | Input -> output | Candidate invariant | Residue/loss | Prohibited promotion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| APHEK3 | Sumerian lexical item -> Akkadian -> Canaanite | referent/category alignment | broken entries, polysemy, grammatical/register differences | equivalence -> genetic relationship |
| XEN | local knowledge -> interpreter/gesture -> Greek reported understanding | reported route/tribute/instruction content | exact source-language words, interpreter fidelity, observed efficacy | reported communication -> verified action success |
| LUC | Syrian ritual -> Greek-language analogy to Troezen | recognizable dedication of hair | local theology, differences in age/gender/vessels, narrator's rhetoric | analogous custom -> common origin or diffusion |

## Inference grammar
`WITNESS -> MEDIUM -> MEDIATOR -> TRANSFORM -> RECEIVER -> CLAIMED/OBSERVED OUTCOME -> RESIDUE -> PROVENANCE`

Do not collapse `CLAIMED_OUTCOME` into `OBSERVED_OUTCOME`. For historical narratives, an author is also a mediating witness.

## Three benchmark checks (to implement)
- **TRIAD-01 equivalence-class discrimination:** classify lexical equivalence, mediated pragmatic understanding, narrated ritual analogy. Must not assign genealogical identity to any.
- **TRIAD-02 dependency/chronology:** chronological distance and genre prevent inferring a direct Aphek -> Xenophon -> Lucian transmission lineage. Distinguish same phenomenon from same chain.
- **TRIAD-03 negative controls:** a fabricated but internally coherent itinerary; a lexical column without a language label; a ritual analogy with no evidence of contact. Reject automatic historical route, language or cult descent.

## Synthesis
`recorded equivalence != enacted mediation != narrated equivalence`. Shared functional structure is a **cross-witness analytical invariant**, not a historical transmission claim.
