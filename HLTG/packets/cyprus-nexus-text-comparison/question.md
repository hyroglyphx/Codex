# CYP-NEXUS Text Comparison Packet

## Question

What linguistic structure can be recovered across Cypriot writing traditions when script ancestry, language ancestry, bilingual equivalence, chronology, object function, and contact are kept as separate variables?

## Calibration ladder

1. **Idalion BM 125320** — Phoenician alphabet + Cypriot syllabary, both readable; calibrates cross-script equivalence and syllabic carrier values.
2. **Idalion ICS 217** — long Cypriot-syllabic Greek text; calibrates the carrier against a known language without bilingual dependence.
3. **Amathus ICS 196 / EC 1** — Eteocypriot + Greek; readable carrier values but unknown Eteocypriot language. Test names, segmentation and morphology without assuming Greek syntax.
4. **Cypro-Minoan Enkomi/Kalavassos corpus** — undeciphered Bronze Age predecessor/relative script environment. Test distributions and function before phonetic assignment.

## Core experiments

### EXP-CYP-01 Carrier-loss calibration
Use Cypriot Greek to measure what consonant clusters, final consonants and phonological distinctions the syllabary forces into CV notation.

### EXP-CYP-02 Eteocypriot segmentation
Mask the Greek portion of ICS 196. Segment Eteocypriot using internal recurrence first. Reveal Greek only afterward and score which segments align with names/roles.

### EXP-CYP-03 Name-anchor transfer
Use independently known personal/divine/place names to estimate the reliability of phonetic correspondences across Phoenician, Greek and Cypriot syllabic renderings.

### EXP-CYP-04 Bronze/Iron continuity
Compare Cypro-Minoan sign distributions with later Cypriot carrier behavior at the structural level only. Keep script-continuity and language-continuity hypotheses separate.

### EXP-CYP-05 Object-function prediction
With sign readings hidden, predict administrative/dedicatory/honorific/etc. function from carrier, layout, find context, numerals, recurrence and document topology. Then unmask text and score.

## Promotion rule

A proposed historical relationship cannot move from proximity to transmission/continuity unless chronology, geography/contact, independent evidence and a plausible transformation path all pass.
