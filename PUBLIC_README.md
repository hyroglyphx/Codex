# CODEX
### What survives the transformation?

**An open computational research environment for discovering, testing, and preserving invariants across mathematics, language, history, and symbolic systems.**

Codex treats research objects as things that can be represented, transformed, compared, tested, and regenerated. The goal is not to make different disciplines look alike. It is to determine when independently constrained representations actually preserve the same structure.

> **Don't just read the result. Run the transformation.**

## The core method

```text
OBJECT -> REPRESENTATION -> OPERATION -> BOUNDARY -> INVARIANT
                                                    |
                                                    v
                                                 RESIDUE
                                                    |
                                                    v
                                                COHERENCE
```

Two complementary cycles organize the work:

**Research-object lifecycle**

```text
Object -> Partition -> Representation -> Operation -> Invariant
       -> Attractor -> Compression -> Regeneration
```

**Analytical pass**

```text
State -> Operation -> Boundary -> Invariant -> Residue -> Coherence
```

The first follows an object through the research system. The second evaluates what survives a particular transformation.

## See it work: ancient inscriptions

```text
Artifact -> Raw witness -> Sign-addressable transcription
         -> Blind structural analysis -> Cross-witness comparison
         -> Holdout prediction -> Recurrence / failed prediction
         -> Evidence-weighted inference
```

The epistemic firewall is explicit:

```text
raw witness != observation != reading != interpretation != historical claim
```

A resemblance is not automatically a relation. A translation is not automatically an etymology. A generated reconstruction is not recovered history.

## Failed predictions are data

Where appropriate, hypotheses, features, and weights are frozen before holdout material is examined. Prospective failure remains part of the evidence rather than being silently rescued after the fact.

```text
ADMISSIBILITY -> SCORING -> INFERENCE -> PROMOTION
```

A result may remain **UNSCORABLE** when prerequisite evidence is absent. Null, contradictory, bounded, and rejected results remain in the record.

## Evidence status

`ATTESTED` · `OBSERVED` · `RECOVERED` · `RECONSTRUCTED` · `GENERATED` · `HYPOTHESIS` · `BOUNDED` · `REJECTED`

These labels identify what kind of object or claim is being inspected. They are not decorative confidence badges.

## What lives here?

- **Mathematics** — modular structure, geometry, recurrence, symmetry, invariants, computational experiments, and formal models.
- **Historical Language & Scripts** — artifact-first analysis, witness separation, linguistic constraints, ancient numeration, cross-script comparison, and blind/holdout reconstruction.
- **Invariant Ecology Registry (IER)** — a common pipeline for representations, operations, invariants, attractors, compression, and regeneration.
- **Codex research state** — provenance-aware records of discoveries, corrections, failed paths, generated routes, and continuing questions.
- **Benchmarks** — calibration and adversarial tests intended to distinguish structural recurrence from retrospective fit.
- **Agent packets** — portable research tasks for Muse, Jethro, and other research workers without collapsing source evidence into interpretation.

## Design principles

1. **Provenance before promotion.** Recovered, reconstructed, generated, and missing material remain distinct.
2. **Independent constraints before resemblance.** Agreement across independently necessary laws is stronger than a single analogy.
3. **Representation is not reality.** Glyph, grapheme, phonetic value, semantic value, and historical relation remain separate layers.
4. **Holdouts beat hindsight.** Prospective regeneration is preferred to retrospective fit.
5. **Failure stays visible.** A failed frozen prediction is evidence.
6. **Cross-witness recurrence outranks within-witness repetition.**
7. **Mathematical elegance does not override source evidence.**
8. **The reader is an investigator.** Expose the object, operation, evidence, and transformation so results can be challenged and reproduced.

## Project relationship

**MikeTateMath.org** is the human-facing laboratory, publication, visualization, game, audio, and educational surface.

**Codex** is the inspectable research substrate: schemas, witnesses, provenance, experiments, benchmarks, relations, and machine-readable research state.

**ARGO NAVIS** is the broader navigation and knowledge architecture through which these objects can become a typed, traversable research graph.

The intended unit is therefore not merely a page or paper:

```text
object + representation + provenance + relation + epistemic status
```
