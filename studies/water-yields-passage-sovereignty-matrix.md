## Cyrus at Babylon — Xenophon

In *Cyropaedia* 7.5, Cyrus diverts the river by opening prepared trenches so that:

```text
river level falls
→ riverbed becomes passable
→ troops enter Babylon
→ city changes sovereign control
```

Primary text:
https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Xen.+Cyrop.+7.+5&lang=original

This is a strong operator match because all three stages are explicit.

---

## Cyrus at Babylon — Herodotus

Herodotus 1.191 gives a parallel conquest architecture:

```text
Euphrates diverted
→ old river course becomes passable
→ Persians enter through the riverbed
→ Babylon is captured
```

Source:
https://topostext.org/work/22

This supplies an independent Greek literary witness to the same Cyrus/Babylon water-entry motif.

Important:
Xenophon and Herodotus are not automatically independent evidence for the historical event mechanics.
They may draw on overlapping traditions.
Count them as separate literary witnesses, not necessarily separate generating events.

## Exodus 14

Operator:

```text
water opens / dry ground appears
→ Israel passes through
→ prior coercive sovereignty is broken
→ new covenantal-national trajectory begins
```

The sovereignty change is political/theological rather than city capture.

## Joshua 3–4

Joshua supplies the closest biblical conquest analogue.

The text explicitly has:

```text
Jordan waters stop / dry crossing
→ Israel crosses toward Jericho
→ Joshua is publicly exalted
→ entry into conquest territory
```

The text itself recalls the Sea of Reeds crossing.

Source:
https://www.sefaria.org/Joshua.3.14-4.24

This is structurally closer to Cyrus/Babylon than Exodus alone because:

```text
water passage
→ territorial entry
→ conquest / sovereignty transition
```

is explicit.

## Isaiah 44:27–45:1

The literary adjacency:

```text
"who says to the deep, Be dry"
→ Cyrus named as shepherd
→ Cyrus named as anointed
```

creates a high-value textual juxtaposition.

This should be indexed as:

```text
DRYING-WATER IMAGE
+
CYRUS ROYAL-ELECTION CONTEXT
```

but not automatically equated with the Xenophon/Herodotus tactical river-diversion episode.

The connection can be:
- poetic sovereignty imagery,
- memory of conquest traditions,
- conventional divine-control-of-waters language,
- or a more specific Cyrus/Babylon resonance.

The present state remains CANDIDATE.

## New distinction — operator depth

Define:

```text
W0 = water alteration only
W1 = water alteration + passage
W2 = water alteration + passage + territorial transition
W3 = W2 + sovereignty transfer
W4 = W3 + named ruler / explicit conquest frame
```

Then:

- Xenophon Cyrus: W4
- Herodotus Cyrus: W4
- Joshua: W3/W4 depending whether ruler-axis is required
- Exodus: W3
- Isaiah: W0/W3 symbolic depending interpretation

This prevents all "water" scenes from receiving equal weight.

---

## New distinction — mechanism versus function

Two witnesses may share function while differing in mechanism:

```text
Cyrus:
human engineering

Exodus / Joshua:
divine miracle
```

So store:

```text
MECHANISM
and
FUNCTION
```

separately.

That is the same calibration lesson learned from:
- different scripts carrying one proposition,
- different lexical forms carrying one operator,
- different boundaries carrying one exposed-child function.

## Transmission ceiling

A direct transmission claim requires more than W-level similarity.

Promotion would need one or more of:

- distinctive sequence beyond generic water-control imagery;
- Cyrus/Babylon lexical or onomastic residue;
- historically plausible intermediary;
- chronological/contact path;
- shared institutional or prophetic framing;
- textual dependence markers.

Therefore:

```text
WATER_YIELDS
= strong structural operator

direct literary descent
= unresolved
```
