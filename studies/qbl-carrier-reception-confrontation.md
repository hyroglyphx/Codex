# Q-B-L Carrier Orbit — Facing, Receiving, Confrontation, Center

Status: ACTIVE
Updated: 2026-09-21

## Core finding

Do not collapse all Q-B-L-shaped forms into one lexical item.

Separate at least three historical nodes:

A. Proto-West-Semitic / wider Proto-Semitic *qbl:
FACE / BE IN FRONT OF
→ MEET
→ RECEIVE / ACCEPT
→ BEFORE / OPPOSITE
→ RECEPTION / TRADITION

B. Akkadian q-b-l battle complex:
qablu "battle"
qitbulu "engage in combat"
muqtablu "fighter/combatant"

Modern comparative work treats Akkadian qablu "battle" as a marginal East-Semitic survival plausibly derived from "face one another / confrontation."

C. Akkadian qablu "hips / waist / middle / centre":
a separate dictionary lemma/homonym.
It yields:
- body middle / loins / waist,
- spatial middle/centre,
- temporal middle,
- qablītu "middle",
- qablu rakāsu "gird the loins" → equip/prepare.

Its formal Q-B-L shape is real, but direct etymological identity with West-Semitic *qbl "face/receive" is not presently established.

## West-Semitic semantic geometry

Reconstructed core:
```text
FACE / BE BEFORE
→ COME FACE-TO-FACE
→ MEET
→ RECEIVE WHAT COMES TOWARD YOU
→ ACCEPT
→ THAT WHICH IS RECEIVED
→ TRADITION
```

Arabic preserves the geometry especially clearly:
- qabl(u) — before / previously
- qibla — direction faced
- qābala — meet / face
- qabūl — acceptance
- taqabbala — receive / accept
- istiqbāl — reception / welcoming / facing toward

Aramaic:
qbl — receive / accept
l-qbl — before / opposite / against

Hebrew:
qbl — receive / accept, especially productive in later Hebrew and under Aramaic influence.
The older spatial-opposition field is preserved in forms meaning corresponding/opposite.

## Kabbalah

קַבָּלָה qabbālāh:
RECEPTION
→ RECEIVED TRADITION
→ chain/body of transmitted teaching
→ later technical name for Jewish mystical tradition.

The semantic core therefore belongs securely to the RECEIVE branch, not to Akkadian "waist."

Mishnah Avot's opening:
"Moses received (qibbel) Torah from Sinai and transmitted it..."
is an early canonical expression of the transmission operator long before medieval Kabbalah crystallized as a named mystical movement.

## Historical stratification of Jewish mysticism

Keep separate:
1. qbl reception/transmission vocabulary — ancient/rabbinic;
2. apocalyptic / merkavah / hekhalot / Sefer Yetzirah streams — late antique and early medieval;
3. Bahir / Provence — late 12th c.;
4. Gerona/Catalonia and Castile — 13th c. systematization;
5. Zoharic and later Kabbalah.

Antecedent mystical traditions predate Catalan Kabbalah by centuries, but should not all be retroactively called "Kabbalah" in the same technical historical sense.

## Akkadian middle/battle problem

Akkadian dictionaries distinguish:
- qablu I "hips, waist, middle"
- qablu II "battle, warfare"

The battle noun is linked with qitbulu "engage in combat."
Comparative Semitic scholarship plausibly connects this battle sense with inherited *qbl:
```text
FACE ONE ANOTHER
→ CONFRONT
→ BATTLE
```

The "middle/waist" noun remains a separate lexical node unless further historical evidence establishes identity.

Do not use Arabic qalb "heart" (Q-L-B) as proof of Q-B-L identity; metathesis is a candidate transform only if independently licensed.

## Operator interpretation

Secure Q-B-L relational operator:
```text
ORIENT_TOWARD
→ FACE
→ ENCOUNTER
→ RECEIVE / RESIST
```

This naturally branches:
```text
ENCOUNTER + acceptance → RECEIVE
ENCOUNTER + hostility → BATTLE
spatial orientation → BEFORE / QIBLA
received content → TRADITION / QABBALAH
```

Akkadian qablu "middle/waist" creates a second operator:
```text
CENTER / MEDIAN / BODY-MIDDLE
```

Its phonological overlap with Q-B-L should be tracked but not fused.
