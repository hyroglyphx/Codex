## Revised thesis

The newer evidence supports a more precise project thesis:

> Ancient information does not travel as a single object. Identity, sound, script, morphology, semantic address, operator, classifier, episode, relational network, audience, and institutional setting have partially independent transmission histories. The task of the Nexus is to measure which channels covary, which decouple, and which transformations successfully predict unseen evidence.

That thesis is stronger than either:
- whole-word etymology alone,
- or free symbolic resemblance.

It is also more falsifiable.
