## Purpose

The Codex now has enough calibration objects that older insights can be revisited systematically rather than merely accumulated.

The recursive operation is:

```text
OLD CLAIM / OPERATOR
      ↓
NEW CALIBRATION OR NEGATIVE CONTROL
      ↓
RETYPE VARIABLES
      ↓
TEST TRANSFORMATION / DEPENDENCE / NULL
      ↓

```

The goal is not to reward age or novelty.
The goal is to ask what survives better measurement.

