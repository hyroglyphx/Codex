# SA / SAR Liminal Carrier Cluster v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** ACTIVE CANDIDATE / SOURCE-LOCKED FIRST PASS  
**Scope:** Samothrace, Samos/Saonnesus/Saon/Saos/Saii, Sardis/Sfard/Sparda/Sepharad, Sardinia/Sardus, Sauromatae/Sarmatae, liminal geography, phonemic carrier adaptation

## Research question

Does the recurring `SA-/SAR-/SAU-` onset in ancient names behave as:

1. one historical morpheme;
2. several unrelated roots occupying a common phonotactic window;
3. a receiving-language carrier shape repeatedly attached to liminal/geographic classifiers;
4. a mixture of inherited names, exonyms, folk etymologies and narrative rebinding?

This study does **not** assume a universal SA prefix. It treats `SA` as a carrier window analogous to the existing CA/KA and BR inquiries.

## 1. Samothrace is the strongest local SA cluster

Source-locked forms/traditions:

```text
Samos
Samothrace
Saonnesus
Saon
Saos / Saoce
Saii
```

### 1.1 Samos / Samothrace

Homer calls Samothrace:

```text
Samos Thracian
Samos
```

Strabo preserves competing explanations:
- `samoi` = heights;
- name from the earlier Thracian `Saii`;
- later Samian colonization explanation rejected by Strabo as secondary.

Thus the ancient tradition itself preserves multiple semantic and ethnographic parses of the same carrier.

### 1.2 Saonnesus -> Saon

Diodorus says the island was formerly called `Saonnesus`.

Immediately after the flood narrative, he introduces `Saon`, who:
- gathers dispersed inhabitants into one body;
- establishes laws;
- divides the people into five tribes;
- is said to receive his name from the island.

This is a direct mythic-name transform:

```text
PLACE NAME
-> PERSONAL NAME
-> CIVIC AGGREGATOR / LAWGIVER
-> TRIBAL ORGANIZATION
```

It closely parallels the existing Phoroneus civic-aggregation operator.

### 1.3 Saos / safety phonemic attraction

Pliny preserves Mount `Saoce` on Samothrace; later Greek tradition preserves `Saos`.

Greek `saos/sos` means "safe, sound, unharmed."

Diodorus' Samothracian flood narrative has:
- people flee to the island's heights;
- their lives are spared;
- they memorialize rescue with boundary stones and altars.

Therefore:

```text
SAOS sound field
<-> SAFE / SAVED semantic field
<-> FLOOD-RESCUE narrative
```

is a high-priority phonemic-semantic fit.

Status:
```text
NARRATIVE/PHONEMIC ATTRACTION = STRONG CANDIDATE
HISTORICAL ETYMOLOGY = UNRESOLVED
```

### 1.4 Samothrace as passage node

Diodorus and Strabo place Samothrace directly in a crossing/transmission sequence:

```text
Samothrace
-> Dardanus crosses to Asia
-> Dardania/Troy
-> Samothracian rites transmitted onward
```

Diodorus' flood story also explains the opening/bursting route through the Hellespont.

Thus Samothrace combines:
- SA carrier density;
- height/safety;
- flood survival;
- strait opening;
- ritual initiation;
- migration/crossing.

## 2. Sardis is a carrier-adaptation control

The city is not source-locked as simple original `SAR-`.

Attested/recognized orbit:

```text
Lydian: Sfard / sfar(i)-
Old Persian: Sparda
Hebrew: Sepharad
Greek: Sardis
```

The Sardis bilingual and Lydian evidence strengthen the identification of `Sfard` with Sardis.

Important consequence:

```text
Greek SAR-
may represent a receiving-language output
rather than an original SA morpheme.
```

This is highly useful for the phonemic-construction model because a cluster such as `SF-/SP-` can surface differently in another language.

Add control:

```text
SA-CTRL-01 = RECEIVING_LANGUAGE_ONSET_SIMPLIFICATION
```

Do not infer Sardis -> Samothrace from the shared Greek `Sa-` surface.

## 3. Sardinia / Sardus is an eponymic-crossing control

Pausanias says:
- he does not know the ancient native name;
- Greek traders called the island `Ichnussa`;
- the first sailors said to cross there were Libyans;
- their leader was `Sardus`;
- the island was renamed after Sardus.

Thus the ancient narrative itself gives:

```text
CROSSING
-> LEADER
-> EPONYM
-> ISLAND NAME
```

This is directly relevant to the identity tensor even if Sardus is not the historical origin of the name.

A separate comparison between `Sard-` and Egyptian `Shardana/Sherden` remains historically debated and is not promoted here.

## 4. Sauromatae -> Sarmatae is a frontier-carrier control

Herodotus uses `Sauromatae`, not the later `Sarmatae` form.

His geography is explicit:

```text
across the Tanais
-> no longer Scythia
-> first district belongs to Sauromatae
```

His origin story then has:
- Amazons arrive near Maeotis;
- Scythian youths join them;
- the mixed group crosses the Tanais;
- they settle in the land later occupied by the Sauromatae;
- their language is described as Scythian altered through imperfect acquisition.

This gives:

```text
BOUNDARY CROSSING
-> NEW SETTLEMENT
-> MIXED LANGUAGE
-> PEOPLE CLASSIFIER
```

The later `Sarmatae/Sarmatia` form supplies a real carrier shift:

```text
SAUROMAT-
-> SARMAT-
```

without requiring a new people.

Add:

```text
SA-CTRL-02 = DIACHRONIC_EXONYM_COMPRESSION
```
