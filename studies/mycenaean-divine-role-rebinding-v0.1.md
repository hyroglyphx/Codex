## 2. Poseidon at Pylos: prominence is real; kingship is not directly attested

Scholarship can legitimately describe Poseidon as the chief deity in the Pylian evidence because of the scale and prominence of his cult there.

But the Linear B tablets are administrative records, not myths or divine genealogies. They do not state that Poseidon is 'king of the gods' or that Zeus is subordinate.

Therefore:

```text
PYLIAN CULTIC PROMINENCE
!=
PROVEN PAN-MYCENAEAN PANTHEONIC KINGSHIP
```

Add:

```text
DIV-02 = LOCAL_CULTIC_PREEMINENCE_VS_PANTHEON_HIERARCHY
```

## 3. Tn 316 preserves a dense divine network

Pylos Tn 316 includes offerings to Potnia, Posidaheia/Posidaeia, a shrine of Poseidon, Diwia, and other divine figures.

This is important because it shows:

```text
POSEIDON
+ POSSIBLE FEMININE POSEIDON-RELATED FORM
+ POTNIA
+ DIWIA
+ LOCAL SHRINES
```

inside one ritual-administrative system.

The right object is therefore not a simple Zeus-versus-Poseidon succession, but a graph of cult titles, gendered counterparts, local shrines, offerings, and administrative weight.

Add:

```text
DIV-03 = CULT_GRAPH_BEFORE_PANTHEON_TREE
```

## 4. Poseidon's earth-shaking role was not lost in later Greece

Homeric and later Greek Poseidon remains Ennosigaios / Enosichthon, earth-shaker, alongside his maritime role.

So the historical transform is better framed as:

```text
EARTH / STABILITY / AGRARIAN / HORSE / EARTHQUAKE FUNCTIONS
+
MARITIME FUNCTIONS
-> later redistribution of emphasis
```

not:

```text
EARTH GOD -> SEA GOD, old role erased
```

Add:

```text
DIV-04 = DOMAIN_REWEIGHTING_NOT_REPLACEMENT
```

## 5. Poseidon etymology is not settled enough to serve as proof

The familiar interpretation of Poseidon's name as something like 'lord/husband of the earth' belongs to a disputed etymological field.

Use earth-related cult and epithets as independent functional evidence; do not let one proposed name decomposition carry the argument.

## 6. Potnia may be a richer lead than the video recognizes

Linear B repeatedly uses `po-ti-ni-ja / Potnia`, 'Mistress/Lady', with localized or qualified forms such as Asian Potnia and Potnia of the Labyrinth.

This provides a strong structural parallel to other ancient 'Lady/Mistress' divine-title systems:

```text
TITLE: LADY / MISTRESS
+ PLACE / CULT / FUNCTION
-> LOCALIZED DIVINE ADDRESS
```

This should be compared structurally, not etymologically, with the current Hurrian `allai` / Allani / Alašiya inquiry.

Add:

```text
DIV-05 = LADY_TITLE_LOCALIZATION
```

## 8. Zeus continuity argues against the video's strongest contrast

Zeus is already present in Linear B through forms from the Diw- stem, and Hera is also Mycenaean.

Thus the safest model is not:

```text
POSEIDON RULES -> ZEUS INVENTED LATER
```

but:

```text
POSEIDON, ZEUS, HERA, POTNIA, etc. already present
-> regional cult hierarchies differ
-> post-palatial religion reweights and recombines them
```

## 9. Near Eastern influence is real, but 'Zeus absorbed storm gods' needs source-by-source proof

Recent scholarship treats Mycenaean religion as strongly syncretic and shows Minoan/Mycenaean appropriation.

Later Greek religion also clearly participates in Near Eastern contact.

But each proposed divine merger must be tested through:

```text
name
epithet
iconography
ritual
geography
chronology
function
```

rather than inferred from generic storm-god similarity.

## 10. Aphrodite / Astarte / Ishtar is a high-value continuation lane

Cyprus provides unusually strong evidence for later Astarte-Aphrodite assimilation, and Greek Aphrodite's eastern connections are real.

But `Ishtar became Aphrodite` is too linear.

Better model:

```text
MESOPOTAMIAN ISHTAR
<-> LEVANTINE ASTARTE
<-> CYPRIOT GREAT GODDESS
<-> GREEK APHRODITE
```

with different degrees of inheritance, translation, assimilation, and local recomposition.

This makes Cyprus/Alašiya a direct bridge between the current Bronze Age polity work and divine-name/function transport.
