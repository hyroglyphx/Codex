# Δ-001 — Reconciliation / Rediscovery Benchmark

Purpose: test whether the relay architecture can reconstruct a research lineage without flattening provenance, contradictions, or uncertainty.

## Required lineage features

A valid test lineage must contain:

- at least one L0 source/witness,
- at least one user or canonical intervention,
- at least one mutation in representation or claim,
- at least one contradiction, failure, or negative control,
- at least one tested or validated result,
- at least one unresolved residue,
- a plausible Builder-facing next action.

## Ten passes

1. **Recover objects** — identify research objects and carriers.
2. **Recover claims** — separate propositions from observations.
3. **Recover derivations** — identify explicit transformations and dependencies.
4. **Recover mutations** — identify where representations/definitions changed.
5. **Recover rediscoveries** — detect later results that reproduce earlier structure.
6. **Recover contradictions** — preserve failed, partial, and counterexample cases.
7. **IER-normalize** — map each major object to O/P/R/A/I/T/C/G.
8. **Council ablation** — run selected perspective modules and compare what disappears when a module is removed.
9. **Temporal holdout** — hide later evidence and test whether earlier state generates the later result.
10. **Organon selection** — select the next experiment with explicit admissibility and stopping criteria.

## Success criteria

Δ-001A passes only if the system can:

- preserve the strict carrier without silently widening it,
- represent positives, partials, and failures together,
- distinguish genealogy from resemblance,
- maintain chronology and script/language transformations,
- emit a next experiment that could falsify or sharpen the carrier,
- reconstruct the decision trail from ResultPacket back to witness/source.
