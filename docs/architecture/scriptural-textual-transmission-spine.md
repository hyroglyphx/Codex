# Scriptural Textual Transmission Spine

Status: **ACTIVE CROSS-CORPUS SPINE — D4**  
Updated: **2026-09-20**

## Thesis

The Bible/Tanakh complex is uniquely useful to this project because it is not one fixed linguistic object. It is a long-duration family of witnesses that repeatedly transports related semantic, legal, ritual, narrative, and theological material across languages, scripts, institutions, classes, and political regimes.

The spine is therefore:

```text
older West-Asian / Mesopotamian operator field
        ↓
Hebrew / Aramaic biblical composition and redaction
        ↓
parallel translation and interpretation witnesses
        ├── Greek Septuagint
        ├── Aramaic Targum traditions
        ├── Syriac / Peshitta
        ├── Greek revisional traditions (e.g. Aquila)
        └── Latin / Vulgate
        ↓
later vernacular, liturgical, legal, and cultural re-encodings
```

This is **not** a claim that every witness descends linearly from the previous one. The object is a relay graph, not a single chain.

## Why it functions as a spine

Across the witness family we can repeatedly observe:

- one source proposition rendered in multiple languages,
- semantic expansion and compression,
- syntax restructured by the receiving language,
- inherited metaphors retained or replaced,
- legal terms normalized,
- divine titles translated, transliterated, or reinterpreted,
- textual ambiguity narrowed or preserved,
- liturgical language held after everyday speech shifts,
- elite/scribal forms imposed from above,
- vernacular readings growing from below.

This makes the corpus a calibration laboratory for both **synthetic** and **organic** transmission.

## Transmission-mode registry

### ORGANIC
Population continuity, bilingual households, substrate, local speech, dialect drift, intermarriage, migration.

Expected residue:
`sound change | irregular borrowing | dialect continuum | substrate grammar | place names`

### SYNTHETIC
Institutional, scribal, liturgical, legal, educational, imperial, or prestige-mediated transmission.

Expected residue:
`formulae | titles | standardized orthography | calques | translationese | canonical vocabulary`

### SUPPRESSIVE
A dominant institution reduces or displaces a local language/register.

Expected residue:
`lexical islands | substrate phonology | tabooed forms | domain restriction | late archival survival`

### IMPORTED
A language or textual norm is intentionally introduced into a population or institution.

Expected residue:
`abrupt register shift | learned morphology | orthographic conservatism | elite bilingualism`

### CREOLIZING / MIXED
Contact produces a new stable grammar or strongly restructured vernacular rather than simple borrowing.

Expected residue:
`reanalysis | leveling | mixed lexicon | new grammaticalization | reduced inherited irregularity`

### ARCHIVAL / LITURGICAL
A language persists primarily as a sacred, scholarly, or documentary carrier after ordinary speech changes.

Expected residue:
`high textual stability + widening spoken-language distance`

## Vedic external-deep control

The Vedic corpus belongs on the spine as a **deep external comparison**, not automatically as a textual ancestor or descendant.

High-value operator fields include:
- creation from indeterminate state,
- separation/order,
- speech/utterance,
- sacrifice as transformation,
- cosmic law/order,
- flood/survival traditions in later Vedic/Indic material,
- breath/wind/life,
- fire as mediator,
- ritual repetition and preservation.

The correct question is:

```text
Does the operator recur?
If yes:
  inheritance?
  contact?
  shared deep cultural inheritance?
  universal structural solution?
  independent convergence?
```

The operator match may be definite even when the historical transmission route is unresolved.

## Correlation tiers

### T1 — Direct textual relation
Known manuscript/translation/recension relation.

### T2 — Historical cultural transmission
Plausible or documented contact path with identifiable borrowed/adapted material.

### T3 — Shared regional matrix
Parallel forms inside a connected cultural environment without demonstrable direct textual copying.

### T4 — Structural/operator correspondence
The same transformation pattern occurs, but genealogy/transmission is not established.

### T5 — Universal/low-specificity recurrence
A pattern is common enough that its presence alone carries little historical information.

"Definitive correlation" must always state **which tier is definitive**.

## Language loss and class conflict

The spine explicitly tracks social domain:

```text
court
temple/church
scribal school
army
merchant network
household
rural vernacular
colonial administration
legal system
```

A language can disappear from one domain while persisting in another.

Therefore:

```text
language death != total information death
```

and:

```text
canonical survival != vernacular continuity
```

This is crucial for tracing Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek/Latin/Syriac and later vernacular layers.
