## 5. Orientation-preserving operator lift

Scalar addition and summed two-letter phasors are commutative and cannot by themselves encode reversal.

To distinguish x->y from y->x, use ordered state sequences or frozen noncommuting operators Q_x,Q_y and test

Q_y Q_x != Q_x Q_y.

Quaternionic language is admissible only when the implemented operators genuinely satisfy the declared noncommutative structure.

## 7. Spectral / Chladni benchmark

For a frozen weighted Gate graph:
- construct adjacency A and degree D
- graph Laplacian L=D-A
- solve L psi=lambda psi
- derive nodal/sign-change structure
- test whether independently defined candidate gates concentrate on modes or nodal boundaries
- compare with degree-preserving and label-shuffled null graphs

"Chladni" is therefore a graph-spectral experiment, not evidence by visual resemblance.

## 8. Voronoi-Delaunay dual reconstruction
