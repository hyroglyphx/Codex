Status: CANONICAL_RESEARCH_HARNESS

Inspired by: ChatGPT Experiment Analysis skill workflow  
Scope: Organon / Linguistic Core / Ancient Script Lattice / Carrier-Pivot calibrations

## Why this exists

Codex already has strong evidence-state and transformation rules. The missing layer was a uniform **experiment packet** forcing every study to declare its hypothesis, method, controls, results, interpretation, limitations, and next action before promotion.

This harness imports that discipline into the research architecture.

## Experiment packet

Every TESTED-or-higher study should be renderable as:

1. **Question**
2. **Hypothesis**
3. **Competing hypotheses**
4. **Witness set**
5. **Method**
6. **Controls**
7. **Null / holdout**
8. **Results**
9. **Interpretation**
10. **Limitations**
11. **Evidence-state changes**
12. **Next discriminating test**

No section may be filled with invented material merely to complete the packet. Missing evidence remains explicit.

## Promotion gate

A candidate cannot move from CANDIDATE to TESTED merely because a narrative explanation exists.

TESTED requires:
- declared hypothesis;
- at least one serious competitor or null;
- source-locked witnesses;
- declared operators;
- result separable from interpretation;
- recorded failures/limitations;
- a reproducible test procedure.

CORROBORATED additionally requires independent witness, replication, or a discriminating held-out result appropriate to the domain.

## Anti-laundering rule

The experiment report must distinguish:

```
INPUT OBSERVATION
DERIVED RESULT
INTERPRETATION
PROMOTION DECISION
```

Derived results cannot re-enter the same experiment as independent input witnesses.

## First application: ELAM-CP-EXP-001

**Question**  
Can a source-locked Linear Elamite -> cuneiform Elamite comparison recover stable linguistic information across a carrier pivot better than carrier-only or resemblance-only explanations?

**Primary hypothesis H1**  
A recoverable subset of phonological/lexical/morphological structure persists across the Linear-Elamite-to-cuneiform-Elamite carrier change.

**Competitors**
- H0a: apparent continuities are chance/resemblance.
- H0b: proposed continuity is generated by normalization choices.
- H0c: apparent differences/continuities are predominantly carrier effects.
- H0d: witness alignment is overfit to decipherment anchors.

**Witness classes**
- Sb 17 and source-locked Linear Elamite controls.
- independent Linear Elamite recurrence witnesses.
- later cuneiform Elamite attestations.
- Achaemenid multilingual controls where relevant.

**Operators**
BOUNDARY, REORIENT, ALLOGRAPH, NORMALIZE, ALIGN, REGENERATE, CARRIER_PIVOT.

**Positive controls**
- recurring royal/divine-name anchors where independently identified;
- direction/allograph recurrence;
- known multilingual administrative referents.

**Negative/adversarial controls**
- Proto-Elamite Ω: structural proximity without licensed language descent;
- Carian: graphic resemblance != phonetic identity;
- Kaminia: apparent mark != intentional writing;
- external Greek comparison: strong internal chain cannot promote external etymology.

**Holdout**
Reserve at least one witness/sequence from value fitting. Evaluate whether declared readings/operators regenerate it without rule changes.

**Primary outputs**
- recurrence accuracy;
- allograph consistency;
- carrier-pivot preservation vector;
- failed predictions;
- ambiguity branches;
- evidential-firewall violations, if any.

**Interpretation discipline**
A successful internal carrier-pivot result promotes only the internal Elamite relation. External-family or Greek comparisons require independent experiments.

**Limitations**
Record undecided sign values, damaged witnesses, publication/image uncertainty, normalization dependence, sparse lexical evidence, and alternative decipherment readings rather than averaging them away.

**Next discriminating test**
Machine-readable sign-by-sign reconstruction of Sb 17 plus held-out Linear Elamite witness, followed by comparison against source-locked later Elamite forms.

## Project effect

This harness turns Codex studies from accumulating research notes into comparable experiments. A study can now fail cleanly, remain Ω, or promote individual edges without promoting its entire surrounding narrative.
